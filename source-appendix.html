Gimme shelter “Shell companies” are companies that exist only on paper to hold other assets or businesses. Their existence is entirely legal and they are usually used for legitimate business activity. The opportunities for complexity, subterfuge, and misdirection can make them useful in obscuring the identities of those engaging in illegal activity.
