Why Marco Rubio Is Bringing the Clash of Civilizations Back: When Propaganda Is Dressed Up as a Civilizational Cru…

Marco Rubio’s speech in Athens presented American global dominance as the noble defence of Western civilization. Behind the grand historical scenery, however, lies a crude political message: the West must recover its supremacy, Europe must follow Washington, and the rise of competing powers must be resisted. Selective history, Christian nationalism and colonial nostalgia cannot disguise the imperial ambition at the heart of this project. Marco Rubio chose his setting carefully. Standing in Athens, with the Acropolis and the Parthenon behind him, the US Secretary of State delivered a speech designed to look like a historic appeal to save Western civilization. Ancient Greece supplied the grandeur, Christianity provided the moral justification, and the promise of Western decline created the sense of urgency. But remove the theatrical scenery and the message becomes remarkably crude. Rubio declared that the West must maintain its place “at the head of the world” and celebrated the “unapologetic pursuit of national power” under Donald Trump. This was not principally a philosophical reflection on civilization, history or shared values. It was a political argument for restoring American-led Western dominance. The irony is that Rubio appears to believe that dressing an imperial ambition in the language of civilization makes it intellectually sophisticated. It does not. His speech revived an old geopolitical strategy: portray the interests of a dominant power as the universal interests of an entire civilization, then present resistance to that dominance as a threat to its culture, values and historical survival. The question is not whether Western societies possess important intellectual, scientific and cultural traditions. Of course they do.
