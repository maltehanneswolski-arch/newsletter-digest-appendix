Quick Take: the Right Way to Scare People About An AI Apocalypse

I sometimes come across as a person who is not concerned about AIs taking over the world, destroying human civilization and killing everybody, but this is not really because I’m not worried. I’m as concerned as the next guy. I just don’t think hysteria works as an effective tool for propaganda or, as professionals in the business prefer to call it, “public awareness.” As a historian and former professional in the “public awareness” business , I can point at multiple examples of how hysteria failed to make any impact on an intended audience, and how hysterical propagandists failed to stop the outcome they intended to stop. Let me go over two fairly recent ones, regarding outcomes that are still up in the air: the debate over mass immigration in the West and the debate over global warming. The issue of mass immigration has been a controversial one, to put it mildly, for some time now. It’s a massive winner in every opinion poll , and so should be a political slam dunk for those seeking restrictions on immigration. Essentially, only fairly small minorities in the West agree with the idea that people should be allowed to break the law to move to another country, and only slightly bigger minorities agree with the idea that millions upon millions should be allowed to permanently move into their country for any reason, even if legally. Still, neither the left when it was wary of mass immigration as a tool to lower wages, nor the right, in more recent times, have been able to make much of an impact on the intellectual class and the elites, among which cheap gardeners and maids and fancy foreign restaurants are absolute winners, in a late stage capitalism world in which lowering wages is the number one elite priority The people with huge walls around their expensive homes are totally for open borders, and the normies don’t care enough to do what’s needed to force them in the opposite direction. That this is still the case is to a large degree because anti-immigration propaganda has been extremely shrill, focused on the growing chance of racial wars, terror, bloodletting as a result of mass immigration.
