In recent weeks, a new round of tariffs, a new export control on frontier AI models, and a reignited war that drained the Strategic Petroleum Reserve to a 40-year low were each an attempt to manage a strategic risk — and each revealed the same dynamic. In practice, foreign buyers shifted to open-weight models they could run themselves, while Chinese labs continued to close the gap. The lesson was the only durable advantage is to innovate faster.
