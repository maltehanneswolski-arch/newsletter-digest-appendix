In late 2025, HBM was all the rage. You need HBM for AI inference; inference decode is a memory-constrained operation, and only HBM was able to reach bandwidth speeds to serve models effectively. But the market has matured since then, and there is no longer one architecture to rule them all. Different silicon works better for training, prefill, or decode. These companies, including Groq , Cerebras , Positron , Tenstorrent , d-Matrix , Majestic AI , and others, are formed on the thesis that you don’t need HBM for inference decode. Instead, you can design chips cleverly to reduce the need for supply-constrained and low-storage HBM by utilizing cheaper and more plentiful SRAM or DDR.
