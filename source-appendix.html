Keynes, 1930. It’s difficult for a casino to go bankrupt, but under the right leadership, possible. Today’s disruption originates from that same casino leadership in Washington, and coincides with and is amplified by Silicon Valley. The gale force churns like a hurricane, sucking in everything in its path. The result is a credit binge combined with an oil shock. The hurricane forces higher borrowing costs and a wave of inflation on economies that, unlike the US, are not growing above trend.
